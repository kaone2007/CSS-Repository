About the Student Attendance System

Organisation Name

Student Attendance System (SAS)

History

The Student Attendance System was founded in response to a growing need for digital record-keeping in educational institutions across South Africa. Originally, attendance was administered through paper registers and manual spreadsheets — a process that was time-consuming, error-prone, and difficult to audit.

SAS was developed to replace this legacy process with a centralised, real-time platform that serves lecturers, administrators, and students alike. Since its inception, SAS has been adopted by multiple higher education institutions and continues to evolve in line with the needs of modern academia.

Mission

To simplify and digitise student attendance management — enabling educators to focus on teaching, while giving institutions accurate data for informed decision-making.

Vision

To become the most trusted attendance and academic tracking platform for higher education institutions across Southern Africa, setting the standard for transparency, reliability, and ease of use.

Values

  • Accuracy — Reliable data that institutions can depend on.
  • Accessibility — Designed to work on any device, anywhere.
  • Transparency — Students always know their attendance standing.
  • Continuous Improvement — Evolving with the needs of educators.

Platform Features

Everything your institution needs — designed for the realities of higher education in South Africa.

Core Features

  1. One-Tap Attendance Marking
    Lecturers mark an entire class in under 60 seconds. Works on mobile, tablet, or desktop — no app installation required.
  2. Real-Time Dashboards
    Colour-coded status indicators give students and staff instant visibility into attendance records and risk thresholds.
  3. Automated Reports
    Generate and export PDF or CSV reports by student, module, or department in one click, at any time.
  4. At-Risk Alerts
    Students falling below the 80% attendance threshold receive automatic email alerts — and so does their lecturer.
  5. Role-Based Access Control
    Students, lecturers, and administrators each see only what they need, with secure permission-controlled access.
  6. Offline-Capable
    Attendance marked offline syncs automatically when connectivity resumes — no data lost during load-shedding.
  7. POPIA Compliant
    All student data is handled in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).
  8. Multi-Institution Support
    SAS can be deployed across multiple campuses or faculties under a single administrative account.

How It Works

SAS is designed to be intuitive for all user types from day one. Below are the step-by-step processes for each role.

For Lecturers

  1. Log In — Access the portal from any browser using your institutional credentials. No installation needed.
  2. Select Your Session — Choose your module, date, and session from your personalised teaching timetable.
  3. Mark Attendance — Tap Present, Late, or Absent for each student — or bulk-mark and adjust exceptions.
  4. Submit — Records are saved instantly. Students are notified and data is available in reports immediately.

For Students

  1. Log In — Use your student number and institutional password to access the portal.
  2. View Dashboard — See your attendance percentage per module for the current semester.
  3. Check Alerts — If your attendance falls below 80%, you will receive an alert and see it highlighted on your dashboard.
  4. Download Record — Export your personal attendance record as a PDF at any time.

For Administrators

  1. Log In — Access the admin portal using your administrator credentials.
  2. Manage Users — Add, edit, or deactivate student, lecturer, and admin accounts.
  3. Manage Modules — Create and assign modules to lecturers and enrol students.
  4. Generate Reports — Export attendance reports by student, module, faculty, or date range.
  5. Monitor Analytics — View institution-wide attendance trends and at-risk student lists.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Can't find what you're looking for? Contact our support team.

General

What devices does SAS work on?

SAS is fully responsive and works on any modern browser including Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. It supports smartphones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. No app download is required.

Is SAS free to use for students?

Yes. Student access to SAS is provided free of charge by your institution. Institutions pay a licensing fee to deploy the platform.

What languages is SAS available in?

SAS currently supports English. Additional South African official languages are planned for future releases.

Technical

What happens if there is no internet during a class?

SAS supports offline attendance marking. Records are stored locally on the device and sync automatically once connectivity is restored. No data will be lost during load-shedding.

Which browsers are supported?

SAS supports the latest two versions of Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Attendance & Records

How does the at-risk alert system work?

Any student whose attendance falls below 80% for a module receives an automated email alert. The module lecturer is also notified so that early academic intervention can take place.

Can I export my attendance record?

Yes. Students can download their own attendance records as a PDF at any time from their personal dashboard. Lecturers and administrators can export reports in PDF or CSV format.

Can a lecturer correct an attendance error?

Yes. Lecturers can edit session attendance records within 48 hours of the session. After this window, corrections must be submitted through an administrator.

Security & Privacy

Is student data kept private and secure?

Yes. SAS is built with role-based access controls and complies with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). Students can only view their own records. No third parties have access to your data.

How do I reset my password?

Click the “Forgot your password?” link on the login page. A secure reset link will be sent to your registered institutional email address within 5 minutes.
